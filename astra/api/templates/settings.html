Historical replay (simulation)

When ON, the bot replays the last year of REAL market data, one trading day per tick. It trades exactly as it would live — it only ever sees prices up to the current replay day and never the future. This is the honest out-of-sample test: train the models on older data, then watch the trained bot trade a year it has never seen.

Recommended flow: run Training on the full S&P 500 first (that caches the data), then turn this ON, reset the portfolio, and press Play.
