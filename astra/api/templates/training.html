Walk-forward training

Replays years of real daily data so the per-stock profiles accumulate experience before the bot trades live. History is split into a TRAIN window (profiles learn, then persist to memory) and a VALIDATE window (profiles frozen, measured on unseen data). The train-vs-validate gap exposes overfitting. Trained profiles survive portfolio resets.
